Help

      
It doesn't hear my taps

      
Use a hard table (wood, laminate). Take off a thick phone case. Tap firmly, about a hand away from the phone. Raise Sensitivity in settings.

      
It plays the wrong sound

      
Play inside the areas you calibrated, and keep the four areas well apart. To widen an area, run Quick re-tune and tap new points in it. If the phone moved, do a Quick re-tune.

      
It feels laggy

      
Use Chrome on Android. Bluetooth headphones add a lot of delay; use the phone speaker or wired headphones.

      
It plays by itself

      
The phone is hearing its own speaker through the table. BenchPress learns to ignore its own sounds within a few seconds, but a loud speaker can still fool it. Turn the volume down, or plug in wired headphones and switch on Headphones mode in Settings.

      
The microphone is blocked

      
Tap the lock icon in the address bar and allow the microphone, then reload. Inside Instagram or other apps, open the link in Chrome or Safari.

      
iPhone

      
iPhone support is beta. Turn off silent mode, or you may hear nothing.

    

  


  
  

    

      
Privacy

      
BenchPress listens to the microphone only to detect taps. Sound is analysed on your phone, a few milliseconds at a time, and immediately discarded. Nothing is recorded, stored or uploaded.

      
Your table calibrations are saved in this browser only. There are no accounts, no tracking cookies and no server.
